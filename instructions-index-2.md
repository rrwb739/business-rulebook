### 5. Sales (C)
- C1 [a prospect shows buying interest] [Fixed] Close on a call: payment link after the yes, card on file only with the true reason and a separate yes, agreement in writing, read details back, honour terms quoted in error, test payments end to end. (rules-sales.md)
- C2 [setting up the sales process] [Fixed] Qualify before calls and send a true, structured pre-call video and an honest pre-call form; brief and check every one-to-many asset; a second video before any upsell conversation; pre-framing questions with real options only; the pre-call video says plainly what the offer is; good friction over bad, judged on gross profit per pound and closes per seller.; pre-call video promises prices at the end; e-commerce custom route on every product page; count calls to close and move decision information into the pre-call video; offer it as a choice; play it on the call if unwatched. (rules-sales.md)
- C3 [a lead books a call] [Fixed] Contact straight away, book soon, fix show rate first: named confirmation, honest slots, service-only reminders, same-day no-show rebooking; a true prepared-for-you item walked through on the call.; invite sent while on the line, text if anything comes up; many short slots, same or next day, warm no-show message; no-show ladder: commitment check, card held with a fair stated fee, paid consult credited, paid consult not credited. (rules-sales.md)
- C4 [preparing for a sales call] Research about 10% of the call's length, mostly on the prospect; use one fact in the first minute; keep notes factual and minimal. (rules-sales.md)
- C5 [on a sales call] [Fixed] Follow CLOSER (clarify, label, overview, sell, explain, reinforce) with the diagnostic inside it; no ask until they've said why they need a fix; likes and dislikes from past attempts.; pre-handle concerns in discovery, no free full diagnosis, a doubt check after yes, proposals answered on the call (rules-sales.md)
- C6 [presenting bonuses on a one-to-one call] Ask for the sale first on the core offer; add one obstacle-removing bonus at a time after a no; never discount to close. (rules-sales.md)
- C7 [presenting the price] [Fixed] Use the return close with their own cautious numbers, say it is expensive just before the number, state the price and stop; fix belief or the offer, not the pitch. (rules-sales.md)
- C8 [a prospect hesitates, or the call gets lost in detail] Best case, worst case close; "let's zoom out". (rules-sales-b.md)
- C9 [a prospect asks for more proof] [Fixed] Ask how much proof is enough, find the real fear, and offer consented, unrewarded client references. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C10 [a prospect says no or stalls] Resolve the concern, then ask again, while rapport holds; expect a first no; universal closes first (does this get you closer, main concern, reason, hypothetical, zoom out, 1 to 10, best and worst case only with a real guarantee), ask again after each, after three or four noes book the next call; never the reason close on someone who cannot afford it. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C11 [the end of any call] Book the next step before hanging up and ask who else would benefit; one light introduction ask after a no. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C12 [negotiating price or terms] [Fixed] Have an alternative first, anchor high but credible, trade on terms in real steps only; "can you do it for more?", and a lower price only with less scope; the same-price comparison question lets the buyer state why the dearer option is worth it. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C13 [before a block of sales calls, or practising sales] Practise selling like any skill: short daily reps, role-play, a rubric per CLOSER step, call reviews and a don't-say list; judge calls on script adherence before the close.; new sellers trained on good recordings, self-critique first, drill one part at a time; when the owner closes far better, reps set appointments and the owner closes until reps match on the same script.; blackout memorisation; stop-and-correct role-play (rules-sales-b.md)
- C14 [an obstacle, objection or stall on a call] [Fixed] Learn the reason behind each objection type, answer curiosity, acknowledge, associate, ask; keep a true story bank; answers by type; decision makers invited, never pressed against a partner.; questions not statements, agree with the true part, their own criteria, willing to walk away (rules-sales-b.md)

### 6. Money models (M)
- M1 [pricing, offer design, monthly review] Three growth levers; judge clients on lifetime gross profit including the owner's hours; aim for 80% gross margin on services; define the owner's hourly rate; price is usually the strongest profit lever.; the smallest unit must profit at full capacity before adding volume; a written pricing formula gives every quote a floor (full delivery cost plus target margin), value sets the price above it; job or package prices, not hourly; same-day quotes. (rules-money-1.md)
- M2 [setting or raising any price] Price on value, never cost; stay clearly premium; raise only after raising value; never cut price to win; one published price for all; charge for the scarce part; same work worth more to bigger buyers; never cut price for existing clients to answer a competitor.; feeling like posing means the claim is wrong, not the price (rules-money-1.md)
- M3 [setting a revenue goal or a price] Work backwards: goal divided by price gives clients needed; check it fits hours and outreach capacity; compare high-price and low-price shapes on audience, acquisition cost, fulfilment and refunds; fastest route is usually an expensive offer to few buyers; no universal price point. (rules-money-1.md)
- M4 [setting or reviewing the price] Competitor price check, narrowing when crowded, quarterly price review, and four acceptable-price questions asked only of avatar buyers. (rules-money-1.md)
- M5 [first clients of any offer, pilot pricing, discount requests or negotiation] First few clients free for written commitments, referred strangers with a self-paid charity stake; then full price, staged rises, close-rate bands as a price check, notice before rises.; yearly price review against costs and inflation, big tests judged on a set number of calls; a higher price can raise close rate; write the break-even close rate before a rise (rules-money-1.md)
- M6 [pricing, or clients not doing the work] A price that stings a little builds commitment and perceived value; check the price doesn't read as too cheap. (rules-money-1.md)
- M7 [pricing or running a sales call] Three tiers (DIY, core, premium at about 10x) about 5x apart; barbell check; build from the top down; premium listed first; an anchor tier kept close to the core offer when few will buy it, still real. (rules-money-1.md)
- M8 [the core offer is ending for a client, designing an entry offer that leads into continuity, or buyers arrive through a low-price automated sale] Offer continuity as the next step; design it in from day one; the continuity offer is a proper sales call; short fixed-length programme as an entry offer with continuity offered at its visible end; a person offering recent automated buyers the higher next step; education brings buyers in, a sticky done-for-you service or used-up product keeps them; repeated do-it-for-me requests are demand for a fixed-term build then continuity, led with only if retention holds. (rules-money-1.md)
- M9 [designing or running continuity] Find leave points, log cancellations by reason, track retention by batch, separate experience from results, yearly letter.; a learning call with every canceller that never blocks the cancellation; fewer things, higher use, overwhelm causes cancellations; many never start: sell only the profile that sticks, build onboarding from their first actions; joins against cancels; churn milestones (month one, day 90, month six); cancel reasons every 20; lower tier only for ideal members; keep-one survey; busy and deep paths; daily presence; regulars (rules-money-1.md)
- M10 [the core offer's demand depends on something that will fade] Plan and prove the next offer before demand fades, including a window opened by a technology still hard to use well; invest only in needs that last; score candidates on six tests. (rules-money-1.md)
- M11 [a client has just bought] Offer zero-delivery-cost add-ons that recover acquisition cost. (rules-money-1.md)
- M12 [planning the year for continuity members] Short extra programmes for members each cycle; events and members' spaces later, with honesty, consent and no sharing between competitors. (rules-money-1.md)
- M13 [a client asks for something outside the core offer] Stay productised; add features only if they help most clients; decline kindly and log; do what's right without bending [Fixed] rules. (rules-money-1.md)
- M14 [opening a new delivery batch or offer] Pre-sell before delivery; never take money for what won't be delivered. (rules-money-1.md)
- M15 [a full delivery batch is running] One full batch must make enough before adding offers or batches; if not, raise the price. (rules-money-1.md)
- M16 [designing an upsell] [stage: S3+] Price an upsell at about 5x the core price, after the first target. (rules-money-1.md)
- M17 [leads feel too expensive or slow] Fix with higher lifetime value, not cheaper leads. (rules-money-1.md)
- M18 [considering affiliates or partners] Affiliates after the first target at a minimum price; partner bonuses earlier; prefer owned offers with a back end. (rules-money-1.md)
- M19 [spending business money or deciding what to do with profit] Payback test on spend, payment split on arrival, reinvest first, barbell surplus, investment question list and checks on opportunities from others, active money only inside the owner's skill, return on reinvested capital, wealth ladder.; business value grows with retained profit times the multiple, deferred not tax-free; five written questions before any outside investment; concentrate only with understanding and control; size bets to survive a total loss; hold good decisions; borrow only against collateral for income assets after the first target.; keep passive investments passive; unplanned spends funded by new extra work taken on for them, not reserves or consumer debt. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M20 [designing the money model, or reviewing a new client] Cash collected in a client's first 30 days should exceed the cost to win and deliver to them.; design the first transaction so blended first cash beats all costs to the next payment (rules-money-1b.md)
- M21 [free early-client or delivery places are full] Free clients choose to pay or step aside for a paying client; collect their review either way. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M22 [deciding how much to deliver, or making delivery more efficient] Overdeliver first, then systemise; group before hiring; label early extras as temporary; existing clients keep what they bought. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M23 [judging whether advertising or outreach is profitable; opens every Strategy tab answer, instructions.md step 2a] The rulebook's most important number. Lifetime gross profit to acquisition cost: 3:1 floor, higher with each human step; how to estimate lifetime value.; 30-day cash of at least twice the cost to win and serve, the cost of a permanent client (rules-money-1b.md)
- M24 [designing or changing the money model, or reviewing results] A money model is a sequence of four offer types; lead with the best-ratio front end and stack the rest behind it.; after the first target, products from leftovers that add no operation (rules-money-2.md)
- M25 [deciding what to build next in the money model] Build in five stages, one offer at a time until reliable and automatic; simple scales; fix back to front. (rules-money-2.md)
- M26 [checking cash, or before any prepaid or free-time offer] Track runway; ring-fence prepaid cash; daily cash checks early; separate business account before the first payment. (rules-money-2.md)
- M27 [any offer, sales call or refund request] Offer at the moment of need, never push; rollover then refund without a fight; ethics and receiving-end tests; true cues only; disputes and gifts. (rules-money-2.md)
- M28 [choosing or testing an attraction offer] Attraction offers bring cash upfront; the five types plus the independent free version; no small discounts; deep discounts only on splinters.; test one long session against multi-day challenges (rules-money-2.md)
- M29 [lead magnet, first sales call, or the DIY tier] Decoy offer: free stripped-down version beside the core offer; scripts, gating, show-up tests and the first-step metric. (rules-money-2.md)
- M30 [after the first target (F3), choosing the next attraction offer to test] [stage: S3+] Test in order: pay less now or more later, win your money back, giveaway, buy X get Y free. (rules-money-2.md)
- M31 [a client finishes a step, a new problem appears, or the core offer is ending] Classic upsell at the moment of the next problem; timing, previews, bundles, activation points, paid priority turnaround of the same work, capped and only when always met, paid extra cover priced on expected claims, beyond the core guarantee and legal rights. (rules-money-2.md)
- M32 [sales call, renewal, or proposing add-ons] Menu upsell: unsell what isn't needed, prescribe a written plan, ask A or B with both options real, ask how to pay; full total before signing. (rules-money-2.md)
- M33 [presenting price on a call] Anchor upsell: present a real premium at 5 to 10x first, then rescue with the core offer; log reactions to the anchor. (rules-money-2.md)
- M34 [setting tier prices] Read the tier mix and adjust gaps or raise all prices accordingly. (rules-money-2.md)
- M35 [win-back, an upset client, a competitor's unhappy client, or a current client buying again] Rollover upsell: credit what they paid towards an offer at least 4x the credit; win-back steps; voucher play later.; monthly reactivation sequences to past clients, true angles only (rules-money-2.md)
- M36 [any no] A no is to this offer: make another; downsells are trades; never discount the same thing; payment plan or feature downsell. (rules-money-2.md)
- M37 [a buyer says it costs too much] Payment plan downsell ladder from most cash upfront to least; upfront saving of at least 10%; instalments end with delivery.; partial up-front share before delivery, layaway as downsell (rules-money-2b.md)
- M38 [a buyer declines continuity, or for any recurring offer that needs their work] Trial with penalty as a last downsell: success actions, fees per missed action, terms initialled first; troubleshooting. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M39 [price objection after payment-plan options] Feature downsell: remove features to lower price until they buy; levers, 1 to 10 check, minimum package, disclosed barter. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M40 [designing or pricing continuity] Continuity for ongoing value only; bill less often; price below the core offer; something used up each period; no lifetime deals; launches: annual only with a needed premium, monthly after the cart closes; a paid membership trial as a split test.; teaching: charge the skill up front, small continuity for what stays useful (rules-money-2b.md)
- M41 [selling continuity] Continuity bonus worth more than the first payment; prepaid block first; automatic roll-on with a reminder; realistic values. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M42 [offering free time or a discount for commitment] How to apply continuity discounts, cancellation fees and easy cancelling; exit interviews; warm endings. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M43 [long-term continuity terms] Set-up fee of 3 to 5x the monthly rate, waived for a 12-month commitment; investigate if over 5% leave early. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M44 moved to X3. (rules-money-2.md)
- M45 [asking for a review or testimonial] [Fixed] Invite every client to review; success interviews; truthful clips with evidence and consent; no fake people. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M46 [every working day, any "what next" question, plan or result] Daily numbers: update the ratio and pick the next action from it. (rules-money-1b.md)

### 7. Delivery and operations (D)
- D1 moved to X4. (rules-delivery.md)
- D2 moved to X5. (rules-delivery.md)
- D3 [planning or running client sessions that change a client's workflows] [shape: service] Workflows not roles; question, delete, simplify, speed up, automate; small batch first; exceptions pile and manual fallback. (rules-delivery.md)
- D4 [building prompts or AI drafting for a client] [shape: service] AI asks clarifying questions first, trained like a new employee; named human check on every AI draft. (rules-delivery.md)
- D5 [designing or reviewing sessions, training anyone, or turning the owner's repeated work into AI skills] Document, demonstrate, duplicate; a stranger must get results from the checklist alone; delegate, never abdicate; AI skills one workflow at a time, passed against the owner's own output, human review at every handoff; learned only when done in the real situation, praise straight after, then less often.; green, yellow, red task hand-off; if-then decision rules with spending limits; scorecards from costly mistakes; watch, supervised, available (rules-delivery.md)
- D6 [delivery work that repeats across clients] Template it so each new client costs less to deliver. (rules-delivery.md)
- D7 [onboarding a new client] [shape: service] Small promises kept in 48 hours, first task done, welcome video, kickoff goal in their words, one-page map, weekly release; first onboarding step a lawful fast-cash campaign from the client own unused assets so the service pays for itself in about 30 days; client actions tied to bonuses and conditional guarantees. (rules-delivery.md)
- D8 [measuring usage or client results] [shape: service] Count questions at set points; watch "we" vs "it"; no individual staff monitoring; self-reporting triggers for the owner's misses. (rules-delivery.md)
- D9 [deciding what to improve] Open questions on value; design partners; private feedback first; result vs experience issues; "keep only one thing" question; reach, impact, confidence, cost ranking; fixed improvement budget. (rules-delivery.md)
- D10 [the owner's own admin] Use the business's own method on the owner's admin first; D3 order; 30-second logging; proper books reconciled monthly. (rules-delivery.md)
- D11 [client acquisition starts working well] Check delivery capacity before raising outreach; build capacity a quarter before a planned push.; when the owner is the delivery limit, deliver what was promised, route extra requests, change delivery openly on fair terms; a trained backup for every delivery role, funded by price; four supply fixes (price, ratio, productise, hire) and the business each builds; groups presented as better; replace unlimited access with set calls and lifelines (rules-delivery.md)
- D12 [running the core offer's delivery] [shape: service] First win in week one; small rewards; progress against own baseline, no rankings; results round at the end. (rules-delivery.md)
- D13 [training a client's staff] [shape: service] Correct the step, not the person; praise in public, correct in private; peer models; everyone gets a part. (rules-delivery.md)
- D14 [any meeting, session or client conversation, or someone raises a problem] Every session ends in changed actions recorded as action, owner, hours, said back and logged. (rules-delivery.md)
- D15 [once a fortnight while delivering] [shape: service] Compare sales promises with delivery; consistent promise across buyer steps; service standard; go/no-go check before kickoffs. (rules-delivery.md)
- D16 [giving feedback or correcting anyone: client staff, their bosses, suppliers, later the owner's team] Compliance errors first; then true praise, "and", own slip, questions, private, easy next step. (rules-delivery.md)
- D17 [any login, account, payment detail or data the owner holds: leads, pilots, clients] Multi-factor and password manager everywhere, least access, weekly backups, phone-check bank changes, encryption. (rules-delivery.md)

### 8. Team and leadership (T)
- T1 [hiring or managing employees who get leads, sell or deliver] [stage: S3+] After the first target: hire through a measured funnel with structured, fair selection, clear culture, output pay and lawful process; recruiting as selling, fast stages, first-fortnight check-ins, paths as behaviours; speak to each person's main driver; judge skill by the numbers a candidate tracks; owner knows each area's biggest problem and costs, signs off pay changes and large recurring costs.; acquisition under one owner, three leaders, every hire raises the bar, five reasons a task isn't done; commission tied to retention and satisfaction; shared team bonus, first sales hires set culture, hire sellers in batches; pay that works at full capacity, fixing mispriced pay honestly and lawfully; define good in observable, measurable terms; authority matches responsibility; one mediated meeting for team conflict; a stated support period when personal issues hit performance, with X18 checked.; short-term profit for better leaders; national hiring with relocation package; outbound and inbound as separate teams, inbound as a promotion; recruit key specialists from admired businesses; core functions in house; hire for learning rate; group interviews for front-line roles; leaders should teach the owner; commission sales hiring: job ad as a sales ad, group calls, offers in person, shadow then watched; no screening on family status; senior leader onboarding: manager's written plan, honest team announcement, meet everyone, bottom-up learning, early visible work; say-do ratio. (rules-team.md)
- T2 [considering an agency, or paying any outside supplier] Pay for output, not hours; agree one written page first, keep ownership and least access, choose and manage suppliers on evidence; references at the owner's size, load test before launch, never outsource the core without someone who can judge it.; learn-and-bring-in-house agency terms, basic then advanced agency, set the in-house date (rules-team.md)
- T3 [considering a partner or a first hire] [stage: S3+] Only when the roadmap stage allows; a partner must bring measurable value, with exit terms agreed in writing first; profit share before equity; a technical partner when the core is technical; equity by measured contribution, salary for working partners, needed functions hired not given equity, partner red flags; the four goods before any deal (good people with mutual background checks, good intentions, good rationale, good contracts); read preferences, ratchets and vesting with an adviser; sweat equity for a written share of each deal; partner only when the person can't be hired and is liked outside work; a fight over final terms signals a deeper mismatch. (rules-team.md)

### 9. Writing (W)
- W1 [drafting any message, email, post or copy] Write in the owner's own voice and the instructions box's style; never synthetic; reread tone-risk messages as the recipient. (rules-writing.md)

### 10. Legal and tax (X)
- X1 [any action, claim, offer, message, contract, hire or spend that the law could touch] [Fixed] Obey the project's country's law; every claim true and provable; if no block or not covered, say so and stop until checked; not legal advice. (rules-legal.md)
- X2 [sending or planning any email, text, messaging app or other electronic marketing, or building or using a contact list] [Fixed] UK PECR and UK GDPR for electronic marketing: corporate subscribers only, sender shown, opt-out, suppression list, trading disclosures. (rules-legal.md)
- X3 [designing, pricing or advertising any money model offer: free items, trials, terms, instalments, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws or subscriptions] [Fixed] UK rules on price statements, free offers, terms, credit, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws and subscriptions. (rules-legal.md)
- X4 [any offer, deliverable, bonus or advice that sits next to regulated professional work, or any decision on investing, hedging or raising outside money] [Fixed] No regulated advice or work without authorisation; never cover a client's regulatory penalties; regulated advice for investing and FSMA s21. (rules-legal.md)
- X5 [any work, tool, recording, post or incident that touches a client's personal data] [Fixed] Client data stays in client systems or DPA-covered tools; client is controller; breaches reported to the client; insurer told first. (rules-legal.md)
- X6 [making or planning any live marketing, follow-up or partner call] [Fixed] Screen against CTPS, TPS and own list; genuine caller ID; no automated calls; stay within Ofcom persistent misuse policy. (rules-legal.md)
- X7 [sending any letter, mailing or physical insert to prospects] [Fixed] No payment demands for unsolicited goods; never mimic official post; UK GDPR notice ready for named recipients. (rules-legal.md)
- X8 [collecting, storing, sharing, recording or using anyone's personal data: leads, prospects, candidates, staff, attendees or referees] [Fixed] UK GDPR: transparent forms, lawful sources, minimisation, consent to record and share, source told when asked, records, suppliers, security, ICO fee. (rules-legal.md)
- X9 [writing or approving any claim in copy, sales, a lead magnet, a demo, an event, a service standard, a label or client material] [Fixed] Every claim evidenced and true; no implied official status or endorsement; fair comparisons; nothing staged; no disguised pitches; no aggressive practices (DMCC s.228). (rules-legal.md)
- X10 [stating any limit, cap, place count, deadline, expiring bonus or reason to act now, on any page or call to action] [Fixed] Every limit, deadline and reason to act now must be true and kept. (rules-legal.md)
- X11 [offering or wording any guarantee, anti-guarantee, exclusion or promise to pay] [Fixed] Full guarantee terms before commitment; never "risk-free" with conditions; statutory remedies stay; exclusions reasonable; never sell legal rights; paid cover may be insurance. (rules-legal.md)
- X12 [asking for, rewarding, publishing or responding to reviews] [Fixed] Genuine reviews only; invite all; incentives disclosed and never conditional; never suppress genuine reviews. (rules-legal.md)
- X13 [capturing, editing or using a testimonial, case study, client post or illustrative person] [Fixed] Testimonials true, consented in writing, current and unedited in meaning; no actors or fakes presented as clients. (rules-legal.md)
- X14 [publishing any ad, sponsored content, guest post, clip, affiliate promotion or comment that promotes the business, or any content mentioning another business the owner is connected to] [Fixed] Marketing must be identifiable as marketing; paid mentions and affiliate posts labelled as ads; connected businesses labelled "Ad" upfront, never endorsed unchecked. (rules-legal.md)
- X15 [setting up or using any referral incentive, commission chain or referral charity donation] [Fixed] Disclose referral incentives; consent before contacting referred people; check charity and professional rules. (rules-legal.md)
- X16 [giving or accepting any gift, insert or hospitality with prospects, partners, clients or their staff] [Fixed] Token value only, logged, the same for everyone and never conditional (Bribery Act 2010). (rules-legal.md)
- X17 [designing any affiliate, partner or reseller pay structure] [Fixed] No structure paying mainly for recruiting affiliates (trading scheme and pyramid rules). (rules-legal.md)
- X18 [hiring, paying, managing, changing or ending anyone's work for the business: staff, contractors, freelancers, interns, callers, hosts or setters] [Fixed] UK employment law: status checks, minimum wage, written terms, employer set-up, hours, fair selection and fair exits.; control over hours and methods points to employment, UK and US (rules-legal.md)
- X19 [setting tax reserves, claiming expenses or reliefs, or offering prizes, rewards, benefits or share options] [Fixed] Reserve rates with a tax adviser; Self Assessment or corporation tax; expenses, reliefs and benefits checked; a yearly tax-planning review with a qualified adviser once profit is material; AI may list questions, the adviser confirms. (rules-legal.md)
- X20 [choosing or reviewing the legal form, dealing with rivals or members who compete, chasing late business payments, holding cash, or protecting systems and brand] [Fixed] Legal form with an adviser; competition law; late payment interest; FSCS limit; Cyber Essentials and trademark checks.; exclusive deals, below-cost pricing and buying rivals checked against competition law (rules-legal.md)
- X21 [a US owner or US business setting the legal form or owner pay, renting the owner's home to the business, selling investments at a loss, giving to charity, or choosing health cover and savings] [Fixed] US owner tax items on their conditions: reasonable S corporation salary, home rental under 15 days at fair rent, capital loss limits and wash sales, appreciated shares to charity, health savings accounts. (rules-legal.md)
- X22 [selling to consumers at their home or door to door, or agreeing any consumer contract away from the business's premises] [Fixed] UK off-premises rules: written pre-contract and cancellation information, 14-day right to cancel, early start only on a durable-medium request, leave when asked, respect no-cold-callers signs. (rules-legal.md)
- X23 [choosing who to serve, saying who an offer is not for, refusing a buyer, different terms for different buyers, or a public political side] [Fixed] Business-criteria targeting is lawful; no refusal or worse terms for protected characteristics (UK Equality Act services, belief can include political philosophy; US federal, state and city laws, some protect political affiliation). (rules-legal.md)

### 11. Case patterns (K)
How to use: general patterns live in the sections above; a K entry is the specific situation where one kind of business used a pattern, written in general words, with its linked rules naming where the pattern is held. Match the business's model, stage and constraint against each trigger; use a match as an analogy marked "Proposed, not decided", never as proof. Rules in other sections win over K.
- K1 [a local service business with walk-in or inbound traffic but low conversion of enquiries] Scripted first contact, short fixed-length challenge programme as entry offer, membership sold at the end while the result is visible. (rules-cases.md)
- K2 [an owner whose one business works and peers ask how] License or teach the proven system to owners of the same kind of business, only with measured, provable results. (rules-cases.md)
- K3 [a fragmented local market of many small operators and an owner with capital] Buy or merge operators in one area onto one system, brand and back office; later stages only; competition law check. (rules-cases.md)
- K4 [many small buyers in one trade each paying full price for the same supplies] Run a buying group that pools volume for better terms, paid by fee or share of saving; never price fixing. (rules-cases.md)
- K5 [a core product that can be free or at cost, with a paired paid service] Free core to widen the funnel, earn on the paired service most users need; check cost per free user and cash. (rules-cases.md)
- K6 [a low-price job sold door to door or in person, needed again] Win the first job, bundle repeats into a monthly membership so lifetime value pays for the selling; off-premises contract rules. (rules-cases.md)
- K7 [a low-price automated online sale where some buyers can afford much more] Call team contacts recent automated buyers and offers the higher next step; lawful channel only. (rules-cases.md)
- K8 [an agency or service business with constant demand but churn, low prices and labour-heavy delivery] Many vendors for a universal need signals low stickiness; rebuild delivery with checked AI, price consistently, keep clients longer; the advantage lasts while good AI is hard. (rules-cases.md)
- K9 [a generalist professional service competing on price] Pick one client group passing the market tests, publish free practical content on their wider business goals from cross-client insight, sell the core service as the way there. (rules-cases.md)
