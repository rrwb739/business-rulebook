### 5. Sales (C)
- C1 [a prospect shows buying interest] [Fixed] Close on a call: payment link after the yes, card on file only with the true reason and a separate yes, agreement in writing, read details back, honour terms quoted in error, test payments end to end. (rules-sales.md)
- C2 [setting up the sales process] [Fixed] Qualify before calls and send a true, structured pre-call video and an honest pre-call form; brief and check every one-to-many asset; a second video before any upsell conversation; pre-framing questions with real options only; the pre-call video says plainly what the offer is; good friction over bad, judged on gross profit per pound and closes per seller.; pre-call video promises prices at the end; e-commerce custom route on every product page; count calls to close and move decision information into the pre-call video; offer it as a choice; play it on the call if unwatched; FAQ in the prospect's own phrasings; price in the video when sales capacity is short; weekly group presentation for enquirers; FAQ by frequency. (rules-sales.md)
- C3 [a lead books a call] [Fixed] Contact straight away, book soon, fix show rate first: named confirmation, honest slots, service-only reminders, same-day no-show rebooking; a true prepared-for-you item walked through on the call.; invite sent while on the line, text if anything comes up; many short slots, same or next day, warm no-show message; no-show ladder: commitment check, card held with a fair stated fee, paid consult credited, paid consult not credited; small paid first appointment for in-person services. (rules-sales.md)
- C4 [preparing for a sales call] Research about 10% of the call's length, mostly on the prospect; use one fact in the first minute; keep notes factual and minimal; payer and day-to-day decider differ: pitch to the decider's standing and workload. (rules-sales.md)
- C5 [on a sales call] [Fixed] Follow CLOSER (clarify, label, overview, sell, explain, reinforce) with the diagnostic inside it; no ask until they've said why they need a fix; likes and dislikes from past attempts.; pre-handle concerns in discovery, no free full diagnosis, a doubt check after yes, proposals answered on the call (rules-sales.md)
- C6 [presenting bonuses on a one-to-one call] Ask for the sale first on the core offer; add one obstacle-removing bonus at a time after a no; never discount to close. (rules-sales.md)
- C7 [presenting the price] [Fixed] Use the return close with their own cautious numbers, say it is expensive just before the number, state the price and stop; fix belief or the offer, not the pitch. (rules-sales.md)
- C8 [a prospect hesitates, or the call gets lost in detail] Best case, worst case close; "let's zoom out". (rules-sales-b.md)
- C9 [a prospect asks for more proof] [Fixed] Ask how much proof is enough, find the real fear, and offer consented, unrewarded client references. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C10 [a prospect says no or stalls] Resolve the concern, then ask again, while rapport holds; expect a first no; universal closes first (does this get you closer, main concern, reason, hypothetical, zoom out, 1 to 10, best and worst case only with a real guarantee), ask again after each, after three or four noes book the next call; never the reason close on someone who cannot afford it. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C11 [the end of any call] Book the next step before hanging up and ask who else would benefit; one light introduction ask after a no; after a no, one ask for a short paid feedback call, research only. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C12 [negotiating price or terms] [Fixed] Have an alternative first, anchor high but credible, trade on terms in real steps only; "can you do it for more?", and a lower price only with less scope; the same-price comparison question lets the buyer state why the dearer option is worth it. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C13 [before a block of sales calls, or practising sales] Practise selling like any skill: short daily reps, role-play, a rubric per CLOSER step, call reviews and a don't-say list; judge calls on script adherence before the close.; new sellers trained on good recordings, self-critique first, drill one part at a time; when the owner closes far better, reps set appointments and the owner closes until reps match on the same script.; blackout memorisation; stop-and-correct role-play; turnaround: one script first, recordings and part-by-part drills; pick a sales lead who likes teaching; owner must know the process; record calls from the start, best-calls playlist. (rules-sales-b.md)
- C14 [an obstacle, objection or stall on a call] [Fixed] Learn the reason behind each objection type, answer curiosity, acknowledge, associate, ask; keep a true story bank; answers by type; decision makers invited, never pressed against a partner.; questions not statements, agree with the true part, their own criteria, willing to walk away; commodity price objection: widen the comparison with true factors, then the cheaper tier. (rules-sales-b.md)

### 6. Money models (M)
- M1 [pricing, offer design, monthly review] Three growth levers; judge clients on lifetime gross profit including the owner's hours; aim for 80% gross margin on services; define the owner's hourly rate; price is usually the strongest profit lever.; the smallest unit must profit at full capacity before adding volume; a written pricing formula gives every quote a floor (full delivery cost plus target margin), value sets the price above it; job or package prices, not hourly; same-day quotes; everyone knows fully loaded gross profit per unit; live quote calculator; price lock within stated scope limits. (rules-money-1.md)
- M2 [setting or raising any price] Price on value, never cost; stay clearly premium; raise only after raising value; never cut price to win; one published price for all; charge for the scarce part; same work worth more to bigger buyers; never cut price for existing clients to answer a competitor.; feeling like posing means the claim is wrong, not the price; sell a fixed outcome, say why hourly misaligns; price a goal programme by time to the goal; sell the goal; don't blend profit and helping in the price. (rules-money-1.md)
- M3 [setting a revenue goal or a price] Work backwards: goal divided by price gives clients needed; check it fits hours and outreach capacity; compare high-price and low-price shapes on audience, acquisition cost, fulfilment and refunds; fastest route is usually an expensive offer to few buyers; no universal price point. (rules-money-1.md)
- M4 [setting or reviewing the price] Competitor price check, narrowing when crowded, quarterly price review, and four acceptable-price questions asked only of avatar buyers. (rules-money-1.md)
- M5 [first clients of any offer, pilot pricing, discount requests or negotiation] First few clients free for written commitments, referred strangers with a self-paid charity stake; then full price, staged rises, close-rate bands as a price check, notice before rises.; yearly price review against costs and inflation, big tests judged on a set number of calls; a higher price can raise close rate; write the break-even close rate before a rise; price endings and small per-item rises on high volume, tested; from cost-plus to tested value pricing on top sellers, by segment; price rise order: new clients first, then existing with value reminder, reason, 3 to 6 months at the old price and a hardship line. (rules-money-1.md)
- M6 [pricing, or clients not doing the work] A price that stings a little builds commitment and perceived value; check the price doesn't read as too cheap. (rules-money-1.md)
- M7 [pricing or running a sales call] Three tiers (DIY, core, premium at about 10x) about 5x apart; barbell check; build from the top down; premium listed first; an anchor tier kept close to the core offer when few will buy it, still real; fee plus share of the win against a higher flat fee; middle-tier price steers choice; self-checkout price below the assisted route. (rules-money-1.md)
- M8 [the core offer is ending for a client, designing an entry offer that leads into continuity, or buyers arrive through a low-price automated sale] Offer continuity as the next step; design it in from day one; the continuity offer is a proper sales call; short fixed-length programme as an entry offer with continuity offered at its visible end; a person offering recent automated buyers the higher next step; education brings buyers in, a sticky done-for-you service or used-up product keeps them; repeated do-it-for-me requests are demand for a fixed-term build then continuity, led with only if retention holds; repeat-visit services: rebook before the visit ends, membership framed on visit history, prepay with the true saving; walk-in businesses: first-timers marked, surprise ending, personal comeback card, running-start loyalty card. (rules-money-1.md)
- M9 [designing or running continuity] Find leave points, log cancellations by reason, track retention by batch, separate experience from results, yearly letter.; a learning call with every canceller that never blocks the cancellation; fewer things, higher use, overwhelm causes cancellations; many never start: sell only the profile that sticks, build onboarding from their first actions; joins against cancels; churn milestones (month one, day 90, month six); cancel reasons every 20; lower tier only for ideal members; keep-one survey; busy and deep paths; daily presence; regulars; churn judged against the avatar's volatility, a retention milestone after which clients stay, each feature worth the price alone; show the value by pulling the numbers yourself; content that helps clients use what they bought; restate goals each check-in; schedule renewal occasions; paid handover from client success to closer at ascension points; separate save team paid on saved revenue, scripted; escalating contact for unhappy clients; contact frequency by price; offer the cheaper level before they cancel; client moving in-house: offer a paid consulting stage until their team wins; churn as constraint: activation point, pause growth and features, one next thing; client data as a condition of proving results. (rules-money-1.md)
- M10 [the core offer's demand depends on something that will fade] Plan and prove the next offer before demand fades, including a window opened by a technology still hard to use well; invest only in needs that last; score candidates on six tests. (rules-money-1.md)
- M11 [a client has just bought] Offer zero-delivery-cost add-ons that recover acquisition cost; care plan after a one-off high-price result, X11 perimeter. (rules-money-1.md)
- M12 [planning the year for continuity members] Short extra programmes for members each cycle; events and members' spaces later, with honesty, consent and no sharing between competitors. (rules-money-1.md)
- M13 [a client asks for something outside the core offer] Stay productised; add features only if they help most clients; decline kindly and log; do what's right without bending [Fixed] rules. (rules-money-1.md)
- M14 [opening a new delivery batch or offer] Pre-sell before delivery; never take money for what won't be delivered. (rules-money-1.md)
- M15 [a full delivery batch is running] One full batch must make enough before adding offers or batches; if not, raise the price; new location: cost, time to profit, payback, launch and staffing written first; soft open. (rules-money-1.md)
- M16 [designing an upsell] [stage: S3+] Price an upsell at about 5x the core price, after the first target. (rules-money-1.md)
- M17 [leads feel too expensive or slow] Fix with higher lifetime value, not cheaper leads. (rules-money-1.md)
- M18 [considering affiliates or partners] Affiliates after the first target at a minimum price; partner bonuses earlier; prefer owned offers with a back end. (rules-money-1.md)
- M19 [spending business money or deciding what to do with profit] Payback test on spend, payment split on arrival, reinvest first, barbell surplus, investment question list and checks on opportunities from others, active money only inside the owner's skill, return on reinvested capital, wealth ladder.; business value grows with retained profit times the multiple, deferred not tax-free; five written questions before any outside investment; concentrate only with understanding and control; size bets to survive a total loss; hold good decisions; borrow only against collateral for income assets after the first target.; keep passive investments passive; unplanned spends funded by new extra work taken on for them, not reserves or consumer debt; stop any project that reveals an uncapped downside, whatever has been spent; investors' incentives; fund growth from customers; acquisition red flags: customer concentration and an undocumented departing owner; when outside capital fits; return on capital for new units; value follows economics, not the label; tech-enabled only counts if margins, retention or staff leverage change; size bets to survive several misses; long shots only from money that can be lost; three ways to add value (clients, value per client, less risk); multiple levers; turn each risk into a strength; offer to buy the business as a risk decision, terms, day-after plan; hiring timing as a risk choice; loss-making only counts as reinvesting with a written return thesis; plans discounted for execution risk. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M20 [designing the money model, or reviewing a new client] Cash collected in a client's first 30 days should exceed the cost to win and deliver to them.; design the first transaction so blended first cash beats all costs to the next payment; a small first step for cold buyers, main offer at the first result; gift cards and prepaid credit, with the rules on expiry checked. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M21 [free early-client or delivery places are full] Free clients choose to pay or step aside for a paying client; collect their review either way. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M22 [deciding how much to deliver, or making delivery more efficient] Overdeliver first, then systemise; group before hiring; label early extras as temporary; existing clients keep what they bought. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M23 [judging whether advertising or outreach is profitable; opens every Strategy tab answer, instructions.md step 2a] The rulebook's most important number. Lifetime gross profit to acquisition cost: 3:1 floor, higher with each human step; how to estimate lifetime value.; 30-day cash of at least twice the cost to win and serve, the cost of a permanent client; the human-step ratios are floors; aim far higher before scaling a fully manual model; cost to win a client rising faster than ad costs signals bad word of mouth. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M24 [designing or changing the money model, or reviewing results] A money model is a sequence of four offer types; lead with the best-ratio front end and stack the rest behind it.; after the first target, products from leftovers that add no operation (rules-money-2.md)
- M25 [deciding what to build next in the money model] Build in five stages, one offer at a time until reliable and automatic; simple scales; fix back to front. (rules-money-2.md)
- M26 [checking cash, or before any prepaid or free-time offer] Track runway; ring-fence prepaid cash; daily cash checks early; separate business account before the first payment. (rules-money-2.md)
- M27 [any offer, sales call or refund request] Offer at the moment of need, never push; rollover then refund without a fight; ethics and receiving-end tests; true cues only; disputes and gifts. (rules-money-2.md)
- M28 [choosing or testing an attraction offer] Attraction offers bring cash upfront; the five types plus the independent free version; no small discounts; deep discounts only on splinters.; test one long session against multi-day challenges (rules-money-2.md)
- M29 [lead magnet, first sales call, or the DIY tier] Decoy offer: free stripped-down version beside the core offer; scripts, gating, show-up tests and the first-step metric. (rules-money-2.md)
- M30 [after the first target (F3), choosing the next attraction offer to test] [stage: S3+] Test in order: pay less now or more later, win your money back, giveaway, buy X get Y free. (rules-money-2.md)
- M31 [a client finishes a step, a new problem appears, or the core offer is ending] Classic upsell at the moment of the next problem; timing, previews, bundles, activation points, paid priority turnaround of the same work, capped and only when always met, paid extra cover priced on expected claims, beyond the core guarantee and legal rights; advertise one to three entry services, sell the rest inside; an in-package offer, within marketplace rules; what to advertise by entry plus upsell maths; ask how many a year and sell the year; pre-renewal video. (rules-money-2.md)
- M32 [sales call, renewal, or proposing add-ons] Menu upsell: unsell what isn't needed, prescribe a written plan, ask A or B with both options real, ask how to pay; full total before signing; menus ranked by price and margin, first-visit pick, pairings, one rotating special, free-item threshold above average order. (rules-money-2.md)
- M33 [presenting price on a call] Anchor upsell: present a real premium at 5 to 10x first, then rescue with the core offer; log reactions to the anchor. (rules-money-2.md)
- M34 [setting tier prices] Read the tier mix and adjust gaps or raise all prices accordingly. (rules-money-2.md)
- M35 [win-back, an upset client, a competitor's unhappy client, or a current client buying again] Rollover upsell: credit what they paid towards an offer at least 4x the credit; win-back steps; voucher play later.; monthly reactivation sequences to past clients, true angles only (rules-money-2.md)
- M36 [any no] A no is to this offer: make another; downsells are trades; never discount the same thing; payment plan or feature downsell. (rules-money-2.md)
- M37 [a buyer says it costs too much] Payment plan downsell ladder from most cash upfront to least; upfront saving of at least 10%; instalments end with delivery.; partial up-front share before delivery, layaway as downsell (rules-money-2b.md)
- M38 [a buyer declines continuity, or for any recurring offer that needs their work] Trial with penalty as a last downsell: success actions, fees per missed action, terms initialled first; troubleshooting. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M39 [price objection after payment-plan options] Feature downsell: remove features to lower price until they buy; levers, 1 to 10 check, minimum package, disclosed barter. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M40 [designing or pricing continuity] Continuity for ongoing value only; bill less often; price below the core offer; something used up each period; no lifetime deals; launches: annual only with a needed premium, monthly after the cart closes; a paid membership trial as a split test.; teaching: charge the skill up front, small continuity for what stays useful; one-time against consumable columns, consumables priced as if sold alone, payment plans are not continuity, continued education; set-up fee waived for a year or paid with short-term continuity; credit-based skippable membership within consumer law; each billing period judged on its own; structural churners: worst-month price or sell to the steady intermediary; low entry fee plus usage charge for structural churners; small-business recurring services: avoid the labour-heavy middle price. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M41 [selling continuity] Continuity bonus worth more than the first payment; prepaid block first; automatic roll-on with a reminder; realistic values; "for as long as you stay" bonuses; time a loyalty reward just after the usual cancel point. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M42 [offering free time or a discount for commitment] How to apply continuity discounts, cancellation fees and easy cancelling; exit interviews; warm endings. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M43 [long-term continuity terms] Set-up fee of 3 to 5x the monthly rate, waived for a 12-month commitment; investigate if over 5% leave early. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M44 moved to X3. (rules-money-2.md)
- M45 [asking for a review or testimonial] [Fixed] Invite every client to review; success interviews; truthful clips with evidence and consent; no fake people; standing review system from the first client; one link at handover; per-person review share as a satisfaction measure, no quotas or bonuses. (rules-money-2b.md)
- M46 [every working day, any "what next" question, plan or result] Daily numbers: update the ratio and pick the next action from it; track a number only if its change would change an action; measuring improves a step; two pipelines, click to close and close to resell; optimise the dominant number. (rules-money-1b.md)
- M47 [a client near or above 20% of revenue, or a large client on offer] Key-client risk: four fixes (more small clients, lock in with term, notice and proportionate exit charge, more of the same size via a repeatable channel, or decline a chance whale); check top-client share monthly. (rules-money-2b.md)

(rules-delivery.md)
- D6 [delivery work that repeats across clients] Template it so each new client costs less to deliver. (rules-delivery.md)
- D7 [onboarding a new client] [shape: service] Small promises kept in 48 hours, first task done, welcome video, kickoff goal in their words, one-page map, weekly release; first onboarding step a lawful fast-cash campaign from the client own unused assets so the service pays for itself in about 30 days; client actions tied to bonuses and conditional guarantees; one-to-one onboarding pilot, goal restated, quick win live, notes and tailored guides, named contact introduced live, next meeting booked; low-ticket: weekly group onboarding. (rules-delivery.md)
- D8 [measuring usage or client results] [shape: service] Count questions at set points; watch "we" vs "it"; no individual staff monitoring; self-reporting triggers for the owner's misses. (rules-delivery.md)
- D9 [deciding what to improve] Open questions on value; design partners; private feedback first; result vs experience issues; "keep only one thing" question; reach, impact, confidence, cost ranking; fixed improvement budget; listen for the shared problem, fix it, announce in one short message. (rules-delivery.md)
- D10 [the owner's own admin] Use the business's own method on the owner's admin first; D3 order; 30-second logging; proper books reconciled monthly. (rules-delivery.md)
- D11 [client acquisition starts working well] Check delivery capacity before raising outreach; build capacity a quarter before a planned push.; when the owner is the delivery limit, deliver what was promised, route extra requests, change delivery openly on fair terms; a trained backup for every delivery role, funded by price; four supply fixes (price, ratio, productise, hire) and the business each builds; groups presented as better; replace unlimited access with set calls and lifelines; supply or demand triage by the double-the-spend test, with the fix for each and a quarterly re-check; loved delivery full: price or people before format. (rules-delivery.md)
- D12 [running the core offer's delivery] [shape: service] First win in week one; small rewards; progress against own baseline, no rankings; results round at the end. (rules-delivery.md)
- D13 [training a client's staff] [shape: service] Correct the step, not the person; praise in public, correct in private; peer models; everyone gets a part. (rules-delivery.md)
- D14 [any meeting, session or client conversation, or someone raises a problem] Every session ends in changed actions recorded as action, owner, hours, said back and logged. (rules-delivery.md)
- D15 [once a fortnight while delivering] [shape: service] Compare sales promises with delivery; consistent promise across buyer steps; service standard; go/no-go check before kickoffs; complaints of harder or slower than expected: fix the sales script first; support one click away, logged by where it starts; suggested replies from past answers, human-checked. (rules-delivery.md)
- D16 [giving feedback or correcting anyone: client staff, their bosses, suppliers, later the owner's team] Compliance errors first; then true praise, "and", own slip, questions, private, easy next step. (rules-delivery.md)
- D17 [any login, account, payment detail or data the owner holds: leads, pilots, clients] Multi-factor and password manager everywhere, least access, weekly backups, phone-check bank changes, encryption. (rules-delivery.md)

pay that works at full capacity, fixing mispriced pay honestly and lawfully; define good in observable, measurable terms; authority matches responsibility; one mediated meeting for team conflict; a stated support period when personal issues hit performance, with X18 checked.; hire for learning rate; group interviews for front-line roles; leaders should teach the owner; commission sales hiring: job ad as a sales ad, group calls, offers in person, shadow then watched; no screening on family status; ### 10. Legal and tax (X)
- X1 [any action, claim, offer, message, contract, hire or spend that the law could touch] [Fixed] Obey the project's country's law; every claim true and provable; if no block or not covered, say so and stop until checked; not legal advice; selling into another country brings its rules too. (rules-legal.md)
- X2 [sending or planning any email, text, messaging app or other electronic marketing, or building or using a contact list] [Fixed] UK PECR and UK GDPR for electronic marketing: corporate subscribers only, sender shown, opt-out, suppression list, trading disclosures. (rules-legal.md)
- X3 [designing, pricing or advertising any money model offer: free items, trials, terms, instalments, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws or subscriptions] [Fixed] UK rules on price statements, free offers, terms, credit, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws and subscriptions; UK: legal rights never presented as a guarantee or feature (DMCC Sch 20 para 11). (rules-legal.md)
- X4 [any offer, deliverable, bonus or advice that sits next to regulated professional work, or any decision on investing, hedging or raising outside money] [Fixed] No regulated advice or work without authorisation; never cover a client's regulatory penalties; regulated advice for investing and FSMA s21. (rules-legal.md)
- X5 [any work, tool, recording, post or incident that touches a client's personal data] [Fixed] Client data stays in client systems or DPA-covered tools; client is controller; breaches reported to the client; insurer told first. (rules-legal.md)
- X6 [making or planning any live marketing, follow-up or partner call] [Fixed] Screen against CTPS, TPS and own list; genuine caller ID; no automated calls; stay within Ofcom persistent misuse policy. (rules-legal.md)
- X7 [sending any letter, mailing or physical insert to prospects] [Fixed] No payment demands for unsolicited goods; never mimic official post; UK GDPR notice ready for named recipients. (rules-legal.md)
