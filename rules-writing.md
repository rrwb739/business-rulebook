## Writing (W)

- W1 [drafting any message, email, post or copy] Write in the owner's own voice, following the language and style set in the instructions box; plain and concise. A plain, honest, human voice stands out: drafts may be AI-assisted (L7), but every message must read as the owner, with a real point of view, and never sound synthetic. Before sending any message with tone risk (a no, a correction, humour, a reply to a complaint), reread it once as the recipient and cut sarcasm and in-jokes.Edit anything that matters (copy, a page, a deck, a script) in separate passes, each looking for one kind of problem (structure, facts and figures, spelling, tone), ideally at different times of day; the value is made in the edit, judged by how much the reader gets per second, not by length.
