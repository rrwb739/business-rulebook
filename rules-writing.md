## Writing (W)

- W1 [drafting any message, email, post or copy] Write in the owner's own voice, following the language and style set in the instructions box; plain and concise. A plain, honest, human voice stands out: drafts may be AI-assisted (L7), but every message must read as the owner, with a real point of view, and never sound synthetic. Before sending any message with tone risk (a no, a correction, humour, a reply to a complaint), reread it once as the recipient and cut sarcasm and in-jokes.Edit anything that matters (copy, a page, a deck, a script) in separate passes, each looking for one kind of problem (structure, facts and figures, spelling, tone), ideally at different times of day; the value is made in the edit, judged by how much the reader gets per second, not by length. In stories and copy, show rather than tell (the detail that implies the fact), include only details that would change the reader's picture, and raise the stakes by saying what was at risk. In long pieces (a guide, a book, a long page), put a short true story where the teaching starts to feel heavy, and test each framework against new situations until no case breaks it, rewriting from the start when one does. Before publishing anything long, send it to four to eight honest readers from the avatar; change what several of them flag, and treat a point only one raises as optional.
