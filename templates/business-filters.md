# Business filters (F33)

The aim: the best business for this particular person to reach their own monthly target. Answer these one at a time before choosing a business. Each answer strikes out options; compare only what is left (ideally three to five), on L1's market tests and an estimated LTV:CAC ratio (M23), check the target fits the hours, then pick one by the pick-by date. Put the answers in the instructions box.

1. What monthly gross profit do you want, and by what date?
2. Which country do you live in and run the business from?
3. Which country or countries are the buyers in?
4. Which languages can you sell in?
5. Remote or in person?
6. What is sold: a service, a physical product, information or software?
7. Businesses or consumers as buyers?
8. How do you sell: in person, by phone or video call, or a checkout page with no calls?
9. One-off sales or recurring?
10. Bootstrapped or raising money?
11. How many hours a week can you really give it, and which hours of the day are free?
12. How much cash can you risk without touching savings you need?
13. What time to first sale can you live with?
14. What skills, qualifications and licences do you have?
15. Who would you gladly serve for years, and which pains you overcame, professional experience or passions do you bring?
16. Who do you already know: roughly how many people, and in which industries and roles?
17. What proof can you show today: results, case studies, credentials?
18. What assets do you already have: an audience, an email list, a website, tools, software or data?
19. Solo, or building a team you're willing to hire and manage?
20. What do you refuse to do or hate (for example managing staff, holding stock, cold calling, being on camera, working weekends, a regulated activity)?
