# Business filters (F33)

Answer these before choosing a business. Each answer strikes out options; compare only what is left (ideally three to five), on L1's market tests and an estimated LTV:CAC ratio (M23), then pick one by a written deadline. Put the answers in the instructions box.

1. Remote or in person?
2. What is sold: a service, a physical product, information or software?
3. Sales motion: in person, by phone or video call, or a checkout page with no sales calls?
4. Bootstrapped or raising money?
5. Which country or countries are the buyers in?
6. Who would you gladly serve for years, and which pains you overcame, professional skills or passions do you bring?
7. How many hours a week can you really give it, and how much cash can you risk without touching savings you need?
8. What do you refuse to do or hate (for example managing staff, holding stock, cold calling, working weekends, a regulated activity)?
9. What time to first sale can you live with?
