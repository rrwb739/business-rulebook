### 7. Delivery and operations (D)
- D1 moved to X4. (rules-delivery.md)
- D2 moved to X5. (rules-delivery.md)
- D3 [planning or running client sessions that change a client's workflows] [shape: service] Workflows not roles; question, delete, simplify, speed up, automate; small batch first; exceptions pile and manual fallback. (rules-delivery.md)
- D4 [building prompts or AI drafting for a client] [shape: service] AI asks clarifying questions first, trained like a new employee; named human check on every AI draft. (rules-delivery.md)
- D5 [designing or reviewing sessions, training anyone, or turning the owner's repeated work into AI skills] Document, demonstrate, duplicate; a stranger must get results from the checklist alone; delegate, never abdicate; AI skills one workflow at a time, passed against the owner's own output, human review at every handoff; learned only when done in the real situation, praise straight after, then less often.; green, yellow, red task hand-off; if-then decision rules with spending limits; scorecards from costly mistakes; watch, supervised, available; checklist detail by learner level. (rules-delivery.md)
- D6 [delivery work that repeats across clients] Template it so each new client costs less to deliver. (rules-delivery.md)
- D7 [onboarding a new client] [shape: service] Small promises kept in 48 hours, first task done, welcome video, kickoff goal in their words, one-page map, weekly release; first onboarding step a lawful fast-cash campaign from the client own unused assets so the service pays for itself in about 30 days; client actions tied to bonuses and conditional guarantees; one-to-one onboarding pilot, goal restated, quick win live, notes and tailored guides, named contact introduced live, next meeting booked; low-ticket: weekly group onboarding; short no-ask updates on work done; contract defensively, deliver as if the client could leave next week. (rules-delivery.md)
- D8 [measuring usage or client results] [shape: service] Count questions at set points; watch "we" vs "it"; no individual staff monitoring; self-reporting triggers for the owner's misses. (rules-delivery.md)
- D9 [deciding what to improve] Open questions on value; design partners; private feedback first; result vs experience issues; "keep only one thing" question; reach, impact, confidence, cost ranking; fixed improvement budget; listen for the shared problem, fix it, announce in one short message; ask which one thing could go; ; owner uses the product as a buyer each quarter; improve a weak client result from the few practices the best 20 performers all share, only if worth the change; clients vote on problems; test the shared practices on the weakest for 30 days and share results, failures included. (rules-delivery.md)
- D10 [the owner's own admin] Use the business's own method on the owner's admin first; D3 order; 30-second logging; proper books reconciled monthly. (rules-delivery.md)
- D11 [client acquisition starts working well] Check delivery capacity before raising outreach; build capacity a quarter before a planned push.; when the owner is the delivery limit, deliver what was promised, route extra requests, change delivery openly on fair terms; a trained backup for every delivery role, funded by price; four supply fixes (price, ratio, productise, hire) and the business each builds; groups presented as better; replace unlimited access with set calls and lifelines; supply or demand triage by the double-the-spend test, with the fix for each and a quarterly re-check; loved delivery full: price or people before format; short of hires: run recruiting as the business, bonus sized to gross profit per hire. (rules-delivery.md)
- D12 [running the core offer's delivery] [shape: service] First win in week one; small rewards; progress against own baseline, no rankings; results round at the end; low attendance: one-to-one start, book next session in session, reminders. (rules-delivery-b.md)
- D13 [training a client's staff] [shape: service] Correct the step, not the person; praise in public, correct in private; peer models; everyone gets a part. (rules-delivery-b.md)
- D14 [any meeting, session or client conversation, or someone raises a problem] Every session ends in changed actions recorded as action, owner, hours, said back and logged; own mistake: put it right and then some; ; "it didn't work": check each step as taught before changing the method. (rules-delivery-b.md)
- D15 [once a fortnight while delivering] [shape: service] Compare sales promises with delivery; consistent promise across buyer steps; service standard; go/no-go check before kickoffs; complaints of harder or slower than expected: fix the sales script first; support one click away, logged by where it starts; suggested replies from past answers, human-checked. (rules-delivery-b.md)
- D16 [giving feedback or correcting anyone: client staff, their bosses, suppliers, later the owner's team] Compliance errors first; then true praise, "and", own slip, questions, private, easy next step. (rules-delivery-b.md)
- D17 [any login, account, payment detail or data the owner holds: leads, pilots, clients] Multi-factor and password manager everywhere, least access, weekly backups, phone-check bank changes, encryption; public warning against impersonators. (rules-delivery-b.md)

### 8. Team and leadership (T)

pay that works at full capacity, fixing mispriced pay honestly and lawfully; define good in observable, measurable terms; authority matches responsibility; one mediated meeting for team conflict; a stated support period when personal issues hit performance, with X18 checked.; short-term profit for better leaders; national hiring with relocation package; outbound and inbound as separate teams, inbound as a promotion; recruit key specialists from admired businesses; core functions in house; hire for learning rate; group interviews for front-line roles; leaders should teach the owner; commission sales hiring: job ad as a sales ad, group calls, offers in person, shadow then watched; no screening on family status; senior leader onboarding: manager's written plan, honest team announcement, meet everyone, bottom-up learning, early visible work; say-do ratio; career path with title and pay steps and a profit-share route to run a location; afford two or three attempts at an expensive senior hire by raising profit first; product-line units with their own profit and loss and unit profit share; new lines led by their champion; in person or remote decided by role; no staff revenue shares; test new pay on new hires first; priority push: name what burns, no-blame opening, clear other work, steps in hours, same-day check-ins, read-back, visible progress, list and prune everyone's work; share real numbers; full checklist but worse results means the checklist is incomplete; build feedback into slow roles; learn each person's reinforcers; hire through a client-style funnel; sell the best candidates; learn what good looks like before hiring; culture as what is tolerated; instruction detail by skill; paired output and quality measures; recruiting video before interviews; task list and workflows before any hire; raise the standard when work is automated, lawful exits; sales hiring as a parallel funnel; gross profit per seller against cost; ; many rules mean a low bar; titles match scope; a struggling promoted manager moved fairly; ; raise quality with "what would you do with two more hours?"; ; reward over punishment, same-day specific praise; the honest-feedback question; good exits; ; management and expert tracks; one mission decision rule; quarterly results shared; owner in the detail on what matters most; client-success bonus on activation, completion, retention and ascension, scaled to the target range; judge operators on total work removed and owner time freed, never on tasks added; sales role ladders: four levels at about eight weeks each, visible, drop-backs, top level earns a promotion trial; light screening for first-touch callers, half-day call blocks; hiring as a funnel; interviews sized to turnover plus growth; variable pay for non-sales roles: leaders split company and own objectives; self-set monthly goals for front-line roles, to test; contact applicants within a day; scripted interview; a name that marks a CV; group interviews for high-volume front-line roles; four communication calendars (prospects, clients, staff, candidates); commission only on cash received; hire for the owner's next role too; an interview round that teaches nothing means hire none; - T2 [considering an agency, or paying any outside supplier] Pay for output, not hours; agree one written page first, keep ownership and least access, choose and manage suppliers on evidence; references at the owner's size, load test before launch, never outsource the core without someone who can judge it.; learn-and-bring-in-house agency terms, basic then advanced agency, set the in-house date; specialist partners under specs, audits and reviews; own only the critical input; key supplier risk: backup, own the assets, notice, exit charge and service levels in writing; prepayment requests a warning; agency best practices as filter, short results window, talk until calls teach nothing new; bench of contractors for sporadic work, one quality checker; software core built with in-house technical leadership, not an outside development shop; fees tiered to reward what the owner wants; advisers judged on intent and skill; check which figure a cited result is; weigh owner cash after reinvestment. ### 9. Writing (W)
- W1 [drafting any message, email, post or copy] Write in the owner's own voice and the instructions box's style; never synthetic; reread tone-risk messages as the recipient; ; edit in passes, one kind of problem per pass; show not tell, only details that change the picture, stakes; stories where teaching gets heavy; test frameworks until unbroken; four to eight honest avatar readers, change what several flag. (rules-writing.md)

### 10. Legal and tax (X)
- X1 [any action, claim, offer, message, contract, hire or spend that the law could touch] [Fixed] Obey the project's country's law; every claim true and provable; if no block or not covered, say so and stop until checked; not legal advice; selling into another country brings its rules too. (rules-legal.md)
- X2 [sending or planning any email, text, messaging app or other electronic marketing, or building or using a contact list] [Fixed] UK PECR and UK GDPR for electronic marketing: corporate subscribers only, sender shown, opt-out, suppression list, trading disclosures. (rules-legal.md)
- X3 [designing, pricing or advertising any money model offer: free items, trials, terms, instalments, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws or subscriptions] [Fixed] UK rules on price statements, free offers, terms, credit, surcharges, reference prices, prize draws and subscriptions; UK: legal rights never presented as a guarantee or feature (DMCC Sch 20 para 11).; no drip pricing for UK consumers (rules-legal.md)
- X4 [any offer, deliverable, bonus or advice that sits next to regulated professional work, or any decision on investing, hedging or raising outside money] [Fixed] No regulated advice or work without authorisation; never cover a client's regulatory penalties; regulated advice for investing and FSMA s21. (rules-legal.md)
- X5 [any work, tool, recording, post or incident that touches a client's personal data] [Fixed] Client data stays in client systems or DPA-covered tools; client is controller; breaches reported to the client; insurer told first. (rules-legal.md)
- X6 [making or planning any live marketing, follow-up or partner call] [Fixed] Screen against CTPS, TPS and own list; genuine caller ID; no automated calls; stay within Ofcom persistent misuse policy. (rules-legal.md)
- X7 [sending any letter, mailing or physical insert to prospects] [Fixed] No payment demands for unsolicited goods; never mimic official post; UK GDPR notice ready for named recipients. (rules-legal.md)
- X8 [collecting, storing, sharing, recording or using anyone's personal data: leads, prospects, candidates, staff, attendees or referees] [Fixed] UK GDPR: transparent forms, lawful sources, minimisation, consent to record and share, source told when asked, records, suppliers, security, ICO fee. (rules-legal.md)
- X9 [writing or approving any claim in copy, sales, a lead magnet, a demo, an event, a service standard, a label or client material] [Fixed] Every claim evidenced and true; no implied official status or endorsement; fair comparisons; nothing staged; no disguised pitches; no aggressive practices (DMCC s.228). (rules-legal.md)
- X10 [stating any limit, cap, place count, deadline, expiring bonus or reason to act now, on any page or call to action] [Fixed] Every limit, deadline and reason to act now must be true and kept. (rules-legal.md)
- X11 [offering or wording any guarantee, anti-guarantee, exclusion or promise to pay] [Fixed] Full guarantee terms before commitment; never "risk-free" with conditions; statutory remedies stay; exclusions reasonable; never sell legal rights; paid cover may be insurance. (rules-legal.md)
- X12 [asking for, rewarding, publishing or responding to reviews] [Fixed] Genuine reviews only; invite all; incentives disclosed and never conditional; never suppress genuine reviews; US FTC review rule; platforms ban any review incentive. (rules-legal.md)
- X13 [capturing, editing or using a testimonial, case study, client post or illustrative person] [Fixed] Testimonials true, consented in writing, current and unedited in meaning; no actors or fakes presented as clients. (rules-legal.md)
- X14 [publishing any ad, sponsored content, guest post, clip, affiliate promotion or comment that promotes the business, or any content mentioning another business the owner is connected to] [Fixed] Marketing must be identifiable as marketing; paid mentions and affiliate posts labelled as ads; connected businesses labelled "Ad" upfront, never endorsed unchecked. (rules-legal.md)
- X15 [setting up or using any referral incentive, commission chain or referral charity donation] [Fixed] Disclose referral incentives; consent before contacting referred people; check charity and professional rules. (rules-legal.md)
- X16 [giving or accepting any gift, insert or hospitality with prospects, partners, clients or their staff] [Fixed] Token value only, logged, the same for everyone and never conditional (Bribery Act 2010). (rules-legal.md)
- X17 [designing any affiliate, partner or reseller pay structure, or licensing the business's name or methods to other businesses for a fee] [Fixed] No structure paying mainly for recruiting affiliates (trading scheme and pyramid rules).; US franchise test: name, control or help, required fee (rules-legal.md)
- X18 [hiring, paying, managing, changing or ending anyone's work for the business: staff, contractors, freelancers, interns, callers, hosts or setters] [Fixed] UK employment law: status checks, minimum wage, written terms, employer set-up, hours, fair selection and fair exits.; control over hours and methods points to employment, UK and US (rules-legal.md)
- X19 [setting tax reserves, claiming expenses or reliefs, bartering, or offering prizes, rewards, benefits or share options] [Fixed] Reserve rates with a tax adviser; Self Assessment or corporation tax; expenses, reliefs and benefits checked; a yearly tax-planning review with a qualified adviser once profit is material; AI may list questions, the adviser confirms.; barter is a taxed sale; promoted tax schemes: plain-words test by an independent adviser, full cost including unwinding, decade-long users, promoter and entity checks, HMRC named lists; deferral is not removal. (rules-legal.md)
- X20 [choosing or reviewing the legal form, dealing with rivals or members who compete, chasing late business payments, holding cash, or protecting systems and brand] [Fixed] Legal form with an adviser; competition law; late payment interest; FSCS limit; Cyber Essentials and trademark checks.; exclusive deals, below-cost pricing and buying rivals checked against competition law; large assets held in separate entities outside the operating business, with an adviser. (rules-legal.md)
- X21 [a US owner or US business setting the legal form or owner pay, renting the owner's home to the business, selling investments at a loss, giving to charity, choosing health cover and savings, or selling shares to employees or borrowing against the business or its shares to take cash out] [Fixed] US owner tax items on their conditions: reasonable S corporation salary, home rental under 15 days at fair rent, capital loss limits and wash sales, appreciated shares to charity, health savings accounts.; ESOP sale is a section 1042 deferral on conditions, not tax-free (rules-legal-b.md)
- X22 [selling to consumers at their home or door to door, or agreeing any consumer contract away from the business's premises] [Fixed] UK off-premises rules: written pre-contract and cancellation information, 14-day right to cancel, early start only on a durable-medium request, leave when asked, respect no-cold-callers signs. (rules-legal.md)
- X23 [choosing who to serve, saying who an offer is not for, refusing a buyer, different terms for different buyers, or a public political side] [Fixed] Business-criteria targeting is lawful; no refusal or worse terms for protected characteristics (UK Equality Act services, belief can include political philosophy; US federal, state and city laws, some protect political affiliation). (rules-legal-b.md)
- US block: X21, X23 (US part); the rest to fill from sources (see rules-legal.md).
