# Rule index, part 2 of 5: Leads and marketing (L)

One line per rule: ID, [trigger], summary, (file). Read all five parts in full on every pass (instructions.md, step 3).

### 4. Leads and marketing (L)
- L1 [choosing, reviewing or questioning the market; every quarter] Score the market on pain, purchasing power, ease of targeting and growth; check the vehicle and any demand window; standing scan of publicly reported problems with a feasibility test; re-score quarterly. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L2 [defining or checking the avatar, or any idea outside the niche] Define the avatar by objective criteria with reasons; turn away non-fits kindly; prefer steady buyers; expansion directions after the first target; go down market only when retention there is strong; choose the expansion direction by the most money made for each client; never done the avatar's job and clients leave: do it before blaming marketing. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L3 [results are poor, or the owner wants to change niche, market or business] Fix inputs, list, then offer (3 offers x 100 messages) before avatar or market; need-to-believes decide push or pivot. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L4 [any outreach or lead generation] [Fixed] One Core Four channel until the first target; messaging apps, follow-up calls, post and events as tested media within it, lawfully.; pick the channel with most skill overlap; do more of one already working (rules-leads-1.md)
- L5 moved to X2. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L6 [setting up or scaling email] Separate sending domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, 30 to 40 a day per inbox; halve volume when bounces pass 3% or complaints 0.3%. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L7 [deciding whether to automate outreach] AI may research, draft and send if each message has a checked personal detail; owner approves templates, samples 20 drafts daily, reads replies. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L8 [the owner asks for leads, a lead list or a lead sheet, or mentions leads in any way] Keep one master lead sheet: research, check names, set columns, fit score from L2 only, lookalikes, batches of 10 to 20. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L9 [building a cold list] Build from directories, registers and websites; prefer public records proving ability to pay and need; test four or five data tools on equal samples; keep the best on replies and cost per sale. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L10 [writing a cold email or first message] Found you, one benefit, ask for a reply; under 100 words, one named recipient, one cue, no pitch; listed tests. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L11 [writing any outreach or copy, or explaining anything to clients or staff] Clear, not clever: below US grade 3 to 5, cut hard, reader's own analogies, plain words, more "you" than "I". (rules-leads-1.md)
- L12 [running outreach] Track every conversation; front-loaded follow-ups each with something new; never contact after a no; avoid the avatar's busiest weeks. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L13 [a lead replies or books] Reply within the hour in outreach windows using ACA; answer quick questions first; handle hostile replies and redirects calmly. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L14 [checking where leads come from] Tag every lead Seed, Net or Spear and keep their numbers separate; compare client quality by entry route; source and referrer on the check-in form for in-person services. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L15 [leads are poor or too few] Add friction for poor leads, remove it for too few; low friction with poor leads means wrong targeting. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L16 [after every 100 messages or 10 calls] No template change before about 300 sends or script change before 100 calls; compare best and worst 10%; one-page call sheet and sales method. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L17 [results are low or volatile] Find what changed before the drop; results lag inputs by months; check input consistency before changing the offer. (rules-leads-1.md)
- L18 [judging a channel or how much effort to put in] Judge by total return and cost per sale, not ratios; credit each step; value lead types by conversion times first-year gross profit. (rules-leads-1.md)

- L31 [a client has measurable results] Ask for referrals and name the referrer when contacting; peer referrals are usually the best leads. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L32 [marketing, messages, calls or planning delivery] [Fixed] Proof beats promise: measure every client, publish results honestly, label pilots and demos, client as hero in case studies; higher stakes need more proof: third-party proof plus live demonstration, captured from delivery with consent, documented free audits when clients are few.; proof capture built into operations so marketing doesn't need the owner's face; rank proof by how hard it is to fake; proof shown where buyers wait; ask how much proof they'd need; only real credentials, no internal certificate presented as outside. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L33 [creating content or posts] Document the journey only with written consent and no client confidential data.; no proof yet: document countable effort, then a waitlist (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L34 [someone turns down a free pilot or free offer] Find the hidden cost or missing belief, remove it, and ask the magic question with price and time fixed. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L35 [contacts who haven't bought yet] Keep non-buyers warm with useful, ask-free notes before key dates; change approach after 3 conversations without a decision. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L36 [choosing or relying on a lead source] Own the lead flow and list; back up single points of failure; log payment disputes monthly; ask before chasing a first late payment; two approved payment providers at full volume; check terms; data portability in writing; list email: worth reading, plain text, easy unsubscribe, segments, click rate, batch-written; quarterly conversion event for non-buyers, tested on a tenth first. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L37 [after delivering value, or a client finishes] [Fixed] Ask for honest reviews and introductions; thank by hand with nothing of value attached; reviews never conditional. (rules-leads-1b.md)
- L39 [any lead or advertising decision] Engaged leads are the output of advertising; offer, then leads, then sales; define "call-ready" and offer those leads a call fast. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L40 [choosing how to get leads] The Core Four (warm, content, cold, ads) are the only ways; usual adoption order; master one before adding another. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L41 [planning daily lead work] Rule of 100 for 100 days; fill the email budget replies first, then follow-ups, then new leads; keep a minimum in heavy weeks; send when the avatar reads, from reply times; zero-budget mix when the owner can't use LinkedIn, can't buy sending domains, can't call or has no warm network: contact forms, about 20 hand-written emails from one free account and L80 warm-ups, refused channels removed, paid sending setup only after a written trigger is met. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L42 [diagnosing poor results] Split advertising problems (targeting, volume) from sales problems; saturation is usually too little volume; intent-based and interruption-based leads tracked as separate funnels. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L43 [a test seems to have failed] Judge only on readable samples (100 contacted for email); decide stopping rules in advance; check every condition before calling a channel failed. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L44 [creating or improving the lead magnet, or building any opt-in, booking or landing page] [Fixed] Narrow complete solution revealing the next problem; seven steps, three types, four methods; simple lawful pages, tested.; paid traffic gets its own page, no extra click; site buttons can point to it; walk the funnel monthly; cold-traffic page layout; sales page: several buttons, a buying-question tool, FAQ, speed, fair comparison, all fees, how to cancel; opt-in placeholder 6.6% median, 15 to 30% strong; immediate beats bigger but slower; quiz or challenge that unlocks a personalised offer; ask only what will be used; separate consent; low open rates: lead magnet value first, then subject line tests. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L45 [presenting, naming or testing the lead magnet] Test headline first with cheap tests from the avatar; behaviour beats stated intent; webinars only after the first target; decide teach or sell before a talk; if selling, give the offer its full time, never a rushed half pitch; one idea per slide with a visual; cut until each section earns its place. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L46 [deciding how good the free stuff is] Give away the secrets, sell the implementation; cost free delivery per customer against lifetime gross profit; say plainly it is free and why; small unexpected gifts sized to the person, no conditions. (rules-leads-2.md)
- L47 [writing any call to action] [Fixed] Say exactly what to do and why now, with only real scarcity and urgency; one ask at a time, easy to act, say-do loops before the big ask. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L48 [cold outreach in general] Solve strangers' three problems in order: list, personalised fast value, more contacts in more ways; sell in steps; it's a numbers game. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L49 [building the cold list] Lists in order: scraping tools, brokers, hand-built; test samples; first 1,000 names; groups are the warmest cold audience; check lists before use. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L50 [writing any cold message] Personalise with true checkable facts, plain text, big fast value first; call opener, voicemail and gatekeeper method; true source and fit criteria when asked where their number came from; standing tests; voicemail callback reasons true and specific; every template read on a phone first; seen-but-silent close-off as a short voice or video note on the channel already used, lawful, no pressure, tested against text; opener: do they already have someone (both answers ready; existing buyers are better prospects); name a nearby client only with permission. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L51 [deciding how much to automate] Automate sending; the smaller the universe the less to automate; research depth scales with deal size. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L52 [following up] [Fixed] Contact every lead more than once in more than one way; stop on request; recontact non-responders after 3 to 6 months. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L53 [cold outreach benchmarks] Published benchmarks are placeholders, replaced with own after 100 to 300 contacts; judge on replies, not opens; later test personal video. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L54 [cold outreach costs and allocation] Cost per customer from labour, software and data, at least 3:1; rep pay, equal allocation at about 70% capacity, quotas, no lead left behind. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L55 [deciding if cold outreach is working, or scaling it] Work backwards from the sales goal to daily contacts, plan up to twice the straight-line figure; replicate the best before iterating. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L56 [contacting people who already know the owner: replied prospects, pilots, members, opted-in contacts; or the warm list runs low] Ten-step warm outreach: list, platform, personalise, 100 a day, acknowledge-compliment-ask, offer, referrals, keep warm; lukewarm list from people who engage with the owner's post and thoughtful comments, within PECR. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L57 [re-engaging the owner's own list] Nine-word email, replies treated as engaged leads; lawful contacts only; fortnightly plain-text email to the opted-in list after the first target.; e-commerce list nurture twice a week: before-and-after, example, FAQ answer; online shop email: behaviour flows plus weekly plain-text email; one objection per email; birthday offers. (rules-leads-3.md)
- L58 [making any content, including sales tools and the lead magnet] Hook, retain (a bank of five true retention devices), reward; captioned and clear on mute; make it for strangers (who the owner is, why listen, no inside jokes); proof ladder; clear titles; production that aids understanding; repost past winners only when better or meaningfully changed, never cosmetic edits to evade duplicate detection; document rather than teach until proven; judge by avatar opt-ins, calls and sales; framework from what all the best examples share; local business: content mainly builds trust for leads from other channels. (rules-leads-3b.md)
- L59 [using content alongside cold outreach] Master list of content by problem; point to exact proof; repeat in new formats; log questions into content, FAQs and playbook. (rules-leads-3b.md)
- L60 [if content becomes a channel after the first target] [stage: S3+] Give well above 3:1 before asking (ads count as asks), asks off-video in short form and for the personalised version, consented keyword automation, text-first testing, owner-as-face decided on purpose, fixed cadences (a repeatable default-format series to sustain them), judge by avatar results not reach; platform test-to-non-followers features for hooks and topics, compared with own tests, winners promoted with the platform's share option, not a second copy; later methods for a content channel.; repeat the core messages, reminding beats teaching; - L61 [considering or running paid ads] [stage: S3+] Ads only once the funnel converts; four steps, three phases (2x test, kill at 1x), budget plus 20%, lookalikes, organic to paid; ad library read lawfully, structures borrowed never copied, AI drafts with owner approval; hooks, bodies and calls to action built as parts, combined, read by part and re-run against the control; paid clipping as capped paid reach, briefed from the claims file, labelled clips only, judged on avatar opt-ins per pound; launch spend cadence and a buyer-value cap; hundreds of creatives from buyers, clips and re-shoots; problem-aware hooks and many narrow callouts as spend grows.; phone-size check of image ads, organic plus end call to action, cross-platform retargeting, bid on own name, fix page and ads before spend; returns that contradict the accounts mean broken tracking: fix attribution, pause tests, move money to winners; cold traffic converts worse than warm: launch the sales route with the ads; scale limited by creative: variations of winners, winning copy kept, range of real people shown, no AI fake customers; test budget about twice allowable acquisition cost per campaign; ad library research, native formats, challenger framing, everyday price comparisons; retargeting and own-name search; 70/20/10 creative from winners; friction by lead quality; free group as lead magnet; show the avatar in the ad; versions with different people widen reach. (rules-leads-3b.md)
- L62 [growing lead flow from what already works] More, then better, then new; volume first; before anything new, write need-to-believes, budget, downside and what a win is.; judge more on absolute profit above the M23 floor; source share against owner hours; estimate lever gains by channel share, added not multiplied; single-channel risk over about half; nurture and referrals first, then a second channel with one new thing only; agency target and ceiling; spend up to the return floor; local long-tail search; plateau check: volume, then formats, then platforms, then methods in the current niche before anything new. (rules-leads-4.md)
- L63 [testing anything in outreach or marketing] One test per channel per week with a log, fix the constraint, test front to back, 70/20/10, vary the wrapper of winners; a standing 10 to 20% challenger split on large sends; test order: offer, headline, image, then the tools in the buyer's path. (rules-leads-4.md)
- L64 [planning how to scale lead flow] Lead getters (referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates) do the Core Four for the business; start with referrals. (rules-leads-4.md)
- L65 [any client relationship; designing referrals] Referrals beat churn to grow; build goodwill six ways; write the growth loop and fix its weakest link quarterly; three levels of influence (owner says, others say, buyers experience); keep the small promises inside content; measure the word-of-mouth share quarterly.; influence from status, power, credibility and likeness; free branded items for recurring clients, never for reviews; owner-free product improvement: ranked problems, pooled budget, representative test, roll out winners. (rules-leads-4.md)
